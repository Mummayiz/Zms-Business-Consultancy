Clear space

  • Keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the “Z”.
  • Nav bar uses the mark + ZMS only; full wording appears in the hero and footer.

Minimum size

  • Primary lockup: 120 px wide on screen.
  • Horizontal: 180 px wide.
  • Mark alone: 32 px. For favicons and stamps, use the flat version.

Don’t

  • Redraw, stretch or add extra effects — use the supplied files only.
  • Place it over busy photos or the skyline render.
  • Use the dark version on navy, or the light version on ivory.
